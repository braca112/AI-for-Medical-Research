# AI-for-Medical-Research

A curated collection of AI research projects in biomedical domains.

**Author:** Branislav Ceperkovic  
Medical Doctor | AI in Medicine | Health Data Science (University of Edinburgh)  
Zenodo DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19125673

### Projects Included
- **Zero-Shot Motor Imagery EEG Classification**  
  - Calibration-free BCI using deep learning and zero-shot learning  
  - Zenodo preprint DOI above
- **Transformer-Based Autoencoder for EEG/ECoG Denoising**  
  - Robust feature extraction from non-stationary brain signals
- **Personalized Breast Cancer Optimization**  
  - Deep Q-Learning for therapy planning
- **Additional AI in Medicine Experiments**  
  - Data-driven analysis of clinical datasets  
  - Proof-of-concept models for intelligent healthcare systems

### Vision
This repository aims to consolidate my research in AI for medical applications, providing reproducible code, notebooks, and preprints to accelerate AI-driven healthcare innovations.
